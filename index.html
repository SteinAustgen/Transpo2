Travel Guide

Tips and reminders for a smoother commute across Pampanga.

1
Visual Landmark Guidance

Verify drop-off landmarks in the visual cue section before your stop. Tell the driver the landmark name or show the photo when alighting.

2
Keep Your Fare Ready

Have your PamSU student ID ready to present before handing over the discounted fare. Exact change is always appreciated.

3
Peak Hour Advisory

Routes fill up fast from 6–8 AM and 4–6 PM. Arrive at terminals 15 minutes early for a seat during rush hours.

4
Stay Updated

Tap "Sync" in the header anytime to pull the latest routes and fares from the community masterlist sheet.
